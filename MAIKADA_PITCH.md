# Maikada Cafe: paid pilot proposal

## WhatsApp message

Assalam o Alaikum. I’ve finished a 3D capture of Maikada Cafe and built an interactive browser preview. I’d like to show you a 2-minute demo. Customers could explore the cafe from your Instagram bio, Google profile, or WhatsApp before visiting. If you like it, I can deliver a branded shareable tour for **PKR 35,000** as a launch pilot, including the final walkthrough page and three months of hosting. Could I show it to you this week?

## During the meeting

1. Open the live tour on their phone. Let them control it.
2. Ask: “Where do customers currently see the cafe before visiting?”
3. Ask: “Would you use this in Instagram, WhatsApp or your website?”
4. Offer a paid pilot with a clear delivery date and one decision maker.

## Pilot terms to propose

- Deliverable: branded Maikada page, interactive 3D preview, booking/contact button, shareable link.
- Price hypothesis: **PKR 35,000**, with 50% on acceptance and 50% on delivery.
- Includes: one review round and three months of hosting.
- Excludes: extra scanning, video production, floor plans and custom website integration. Quote those separately.
- Publish only after they approve the final page and the use of their name and imagery.

If they object to the price, ask what result would make it worth paying for. Avoid promising more features before learning the objection. The scan is already complete; the remaining paid work is preparation, branding, publication and support.
