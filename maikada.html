THE PROJECT

A real place, ready to share.

The challenge

How can a remote visitor understand the atmosphere and layout of a real cafe through a shareable link? This project explores that question with a capture of Maikada Cafe, without assuming a customer problem or sales outcome.

The solution

We captured the physical space and processed the scan into a photorealistic 3D scene. A browser page makes the scene accessible without installing an app.

The experience

Visitors can open the 3D preview, look around, orbit the scene and zoom in on details at their own pace.

The technology

The original Gaussian-splat PLY is preserved as the master. Browser-ready versions are derived from it so the scene can be shared online while the source remains available for future improvements.

The result so far

A working 3D preview and this project page are available. A higher-detail streamed viewer is in testing. We have not measured booking, sales or visitor-impact outcomes for this project.
